The Techno-Commercial Advantage

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science (Data Science) student at Trident Academy of Technology, Bhubaneswar (BPUT, 2024–2028) — shipping real products while most students are still debugging hello-world.

Growing up in a defence family forged something most engineers lack: iron discipline, pressure-tested adaptability, and a non-negotiable sense of accountability. Deadlines are not suggestions in my world.

My edge is what I call the Techno-Commercial advantage. While peers let GitHub repos collect dust, I walk into client meetings, pitch digital solutions in business language, build the product, and measure success by the client's ROI — not just whether it compiles.

I've built solutions for local businesses in Angul, participated in IIT Bhubaneswar's PRAVAAH technical fest, and engaged in startup ideation through the B-Plan event. Every line of code I write has a real-world purpose.
